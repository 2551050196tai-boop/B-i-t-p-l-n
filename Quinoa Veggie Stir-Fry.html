RECIPE

Quinoa Veggie Stir-Fry

Welcome to Cooks Delight, where culinary dreams come alive! Today, we embark on a journey of fresh flavors with a dish that promises to elevate your dining experience – our Quinoa Veggie Stir-Fry.

30 MIN

EASY PREP

3 SERVES

Every bite brings together vibrant flavors and satisfying textures, creating a wholesome dish that is both nutritious and delicious. LET'S GO OVER THE BASICS – THE DO'S, AND THE DON'TS – FOR MAKING QUINOA VEGGIE STIR-FRY

DO'S:

Rinse the Quinoa:
Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water before cooking to remove its natural bitterness.

Keep the Vegetables Crisp:
Stir-fry the vegetables over medium-high heat so they remain colorful, tender, and slightly crunchy.

Season Gradually:
Add soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil gradually to create a balanced savory flavor.

DON'TS:

Overcook the Quinoa:
Avoid cooking the quinoa for too long, as it can become mushy instead of light and fluffy.

Overcrowd the Pan:
Give the vegetables enough space to cook evenly and develop a delicious roasted flavor.

Add Too Much Sauce:
Use just enough sauce to coat the quinoa and vegetables without making the dish soggy.

INSTRUCTIONS

This recipe goes beyond the basics, bringing together fluffy quinoa, colorful vegetables, and savory seasonings to create a wholesome and satisfying meal. Every ingredient adds its own texture and flavor, resulting in a dish that is both nutritious and delicious.

Take your time preparing each ingredient before you begin cooking. Once the vegetables hit the hot pan, their natural aromas begin to fill the kitchen, creating an irresistible invitation to enjoy a fresh and flavorful meal.

PREPARE THE QUINOA
  • Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under cold water.
  • Cook the quinoa according to the package instructions until light and fluffy.
  • Set aside and allow the quinoa to cool slightly before stir-frying.
PREPARE THE VEGETABLES
  • Chop the vegetables into small, evenly sized pieces.
  • Mince the garlic and grate the fresh ginger.
  • Prepare all ingredients before heating the pan for quick and even cooking.
SAUTÉ THE AROMATICS
  • Heat a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat and add a small amount of oil.
  • Add minced garlic and fresh ginger, then sauté briefly until fragrant.
  • Be careful not to burn the garlic, as it can create a bitter flavor.
STIR-FRY THE VEGETABLES
  • Add the carrots, broccoli, and bell peppers to the hot pan.
  • Stir-fry for several minutes until the vegetables become tender while remaining slightly crisp.
  • Add the green onions and continue cooking for another minute.
BRING EVERYTHING TOGETHER
  • Add the cooked quinoa to the skillet with the sautéed vegetables.
  • Pour in the soy sauce and add a small amount of sesame oil.
  • Toss everything together until the quinoa is evenly coated and heated through.
  • Taste and adjust the seasoning with additional soy sauce or black pepper if needed.
FINISH WITH FRESH FLAVORS
  • Add chopped green onions or fresh herbs for a bright finishing touch.
  • Sprinkle with sesame seeds for extra texture and flavor.
  • Serve immediately while the quinoa and vegetables are warm.
MAKE IT EXTRA SPECIAL
  • Garnish the stir-fry with fresh green onions and toasted sesame seeds.
  • Add a small drizzle of sesame oil just before serving for extra aroma.
  • For a little heat, add red pepper flakes or a dash of chili sauce.
Pairing Suggestions
  1. Side Dish: Serve alongside a fresh cucumber salad, steamed edamame, or a light vegetable soup.
  2. Topping: Add sliced avocado, toasted sesame seeds, or fresh green onions for extra flavor and texture.

Roasted Vegetables:Pair with roasted seasonal vegetables for an even heartier and more colorful meal.

Fresh Salad:A crisp green salad with a light citrus dressing provides a refreshing contrast to the savory stir-fry.

Herb Garnish:Fresh cilantro, parsley, or green onions can brighten the dish and add another layer of freshness.

The combination of fluffy quinoa, colorful vegetables, garlic, ginger, and savory seasonings creates a wholesome stir-fry that is both comforting and satisfying. The quinoa provides a deliciously nutty base, while the crisp vegetables add vibrant color, texture, and freshness to every bite.

Whether you're preparing a quick weekday lunch, a healthy family dinner, or a colorful centerpiece for a casual gathering, this Quinoa Veggie Stir-Fry is a versatile choice that is simple to prepare and full of flavor. Its combination of wholesome ingredients and vibrant vegetables makes it an easy meal to enjoy any day of the week.

Simple ingredients, fresh vegetables, and delicious flavors come together to create a meal that proves healthy food can be both satisfying and exciting.

INGREDIENTS
  • 1 cup/190g Quinoa (soaked for about 30 minutes)
  • 3 Tbsp Olive oil
  • 1+1/2 cup 200g Onion
  • 1+1/2 Tbsp Garlic or to taste - finely chopped
  • 1/2 Tbsp Ginger or to taste - finely chopped
  • 1/2 Tsp Turmeric
  • 1 Tsp Paprika (NOT SMOKED)
  • 1 Tsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 Tsp Ground Coriander
  • 1/4 Tsp Cayenne Pepper (Optional)
  • Salt to taste (I have added total 1/4 + 3/4 teaspoon of pink Himalayan salt)
  • 150g / 1 cup Carrots
  • 130g / 1 cup Green Beans
  • 200g / 1 cup Tomatoes - chopped
  • 70g / 1/2 cup Frozen Green Peas
  • 325ml / 1+1/3 cup Water OR AS REQUIRED (👉 Sometimes one batch of quinoa can be more dry than the other, SO ADJUST THE COOKING WATER QUANTITY ACCORDINGLY)
  • 100g / 3+1/2 cup Spinach - LEAVES ONLY (I have added baby spinach)
  • Lemon juice to taste (I have added 1/2 tablespoon)
  • Ground Black Pepper to taste (I have added 1/2 teaspoon)
  • 1/2 cup / 70g Cashews (Toasted)
  • 2 Tablespoon / 5g Cilantro (Coriander leaves) - very finely chopped
