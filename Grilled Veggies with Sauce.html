Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

20 MIN

EASY PREP

2 SERVES

Picture perfectly cooked spaghetti tossed in fragrant olive oil infused with golden garlic and a gentle touch of red pepper flakes. Every bite brings together the richness of olive oil, the warmth of garlic, and the freshness of parsley, creating a dish that proves simplicity can be incredibly delicious.


As you prepare your own, imagine the aroma of garlic slowly infusing into warm olive oil and filling the kitchen with an irresistible fragrance. With just a handful of simple ingredients, this classic pasta transforms into a comforting and elegant meal. Whether you're a seasoned chef or a kitchen novice, this recipe invites you to discover how a few carefully prepared ingredients can create a truly satisfying dish.

LET'S GO OVER THE BASICS – THE DO'S, AND THE DON'TS – FOR MAKING SPAGHETTI AGLIO E OLIO

DO'S:

Cook the Pasta Al Dente:
Cook the spaghetti until tender but still slightly firm in the center for the perfect texture.

Slice the Garlic Thinly:
Thinly sliced garlic allows its flavor to slowly infuse into the olive oil and become beautifully golden.

Reserve Pasta Water:
Save some of the starchy pasta water before draining. It helps bring the olive oil and pasta together into a silky coating.

DON'TS:

Burn the Garlic:
Avoid cooking the garlic over high heat, as burnt garlic can quickly become bitter and overpower the dish.

Overcook the Pasta:
Do not let the spaghetti become too soft. Al dente pasta provides the ideal texture for this recipe.

Use Too Much Oil:
Use enough olive oil to coat the pasta generously without making the finished dish excessively greasy.

INSTRUCTIONS

This recipe goes beyond the basics, transforming a few simple ingredients into a classic Italian pasta dish full of aroma and flavor. The combination of golden garlic, smooth olive oil, fresh parsley, and perfectly cooked spaghetti creates a comforting meal that is both elegant and incredibly easy to prepare.

The secret lies in the timing. As the garlic gently cooks in olive oil, its rich aroma begins to fill the kitchen. Once the spaghetti is added, the flavorful oil coats every strand, creating a dish where simplicity becomes the main attraction.

PREPARE THE PASTA
  • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  • Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente according to the package instructions.
  • Reserve about ½ cup of pasta water before draining the spaghetti.
PREPARE THE GARLIC OIL
  • Thinly slice the garlic cloves.
  • Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat.
  • Add the sliced garlic and gently cook until fragrant and lightly golden.
  • Add red pepper flakes if you prefer a little heat.
INFUSE THE FLAVOR
  • Allow the garlic to slowly release its flavor into the olive oil.
  • Keep the heat low and watch the garlic carefully to prevent burning.
  • Add a small amount of reserved pasta water to help create a smooth sauce.
BRING EVERYTHING TOGETHER
  • Add the drained spaghetti directly to the skillet.
  • Toss the pasta thoroughly so every strand is coated with the garlic-infused olive oil.
  • Add more pasta water little by little if the pasta needs additional moisture.
ADD THE FINAL TOUCHES
  • Add freshly chopped parsley to brighten the dish.
  • Season with freshly ground black pepper and salt to taste.
  • Add extra red pepper flakes for a stronger kick if desired.
SERVE & ENJOY
  • Serve the spaghetti immediately while it is warm and fragrant. The golden garlic, silky olive oil, and fresh parsley create a simple but satisfying combination that makes every bite memorable.
  • For an optional finishing touch, sprinkle a little grated Parmesan cheese over the pasta before serving.
Pairing Suggestions
  1. Side Dish: Serve alongside a fresh green salad, roasted vegetables, or warm crusty bread.
  2. Topping: Finish with fresh parsley, grated Parmesan, or a light sprinkle of red pepper flakes.

Garlic Bread:A slice of crispy garlic bread complements the rich olive oil and garlic flavors beautifully.

Fresh Salad:A simple salad with lettuce, tomatoes, and a light lemon dressing provides a refreshing contrast to the warm pasta.

Roasted Vegetables:Pair with roasted broccoli, zucchini, or bell peppers for a more complete and colorful meal.

The combination of perfectly cooked spaghetti, fragrant garlic, rich olive oil, and fresh parsley creates a timeless Italian dish that proves great food does not need to be complicated. Each strand of pasta is coated with savory garlic-infused oil, while red pepper flakes add a gentle touch of heat and character.

Whether you're preparing a quick weeknight dinner, a comforting meal for your family, or a simple dish for a casual gathering, Spaghetti Aglio e Olio is an effortless choice. Its minimal ingredients and straightforward preparation make it a recipe worth returning to again and again.

Simple ingredients, thoughtful preparation, and bold Italian flavors come together to create a pasta dish that is comforting, elegant, and unforgettable.
